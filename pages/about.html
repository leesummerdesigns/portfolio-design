Product Design Leader, Design System Specialist, Coffee Enthusiast, and UX Mentor, based in Zurich.

I'm Louis, and I was raised in Alicante, Spain, where I completed my international baccalaureate (IGCSE). From there I went to Barcelona, where Elisava University gave me my grounding in communication design and media psychology.

Once I graduated, I headed to Germany and joined a series of agencies. I began as a digital art director and switched entirely to user experience in 2013. Over the following years I explored every corner of UX, always learning and testing new methods, tools and technologies.

In 2017, together with universities in Barcelona and Amsterdam, I helped create an accelerated program for graduates who wanted to become product designers. In 2019, I started the UX Academy at the Cocomore agency in Frankfurt, giving students their first hands-on professional experience.

Today I lead product design from Zurich, Switzerland. At Swiss Marketplace Group I headed a team of 10 designers working on ImmoScout24, Homegate and Flatfox, and moved all three brands onto a single platform. Earlier, at Rakuten Kobo, 37 product teams adopted the shared design system I led, and at Elisava in Barcelona I taught user experience design as an External Professor.

I've worked with startups and enterprises alike, among them Immoscout24, Rabobank, Deutsche Telekom, BD Rowa and Rakuten Kobo.

I'm fluent in German, English and Spanish.

AI is also part of my own design and discovery process: research synthesis, ideation and prototyping, used in a pragmatic and responsible way, with human judgment making the final call.

What I care about is augmentation, not automation for its own sake: taking repetitive work off designers' plates so they have time for strategy, creativity and human insight.

In a nutshell: I have a multidisciplinary skill set that integrates creative knowledge with technical and business expertise. I know today's design tools inside out and pair them with a strong understanding of human nature and behavior. I think critically and keep an open mind, I communicate and facilitate, and I know how to turn new technologies like AI into practical value for teams and organizations.
