What does a Design System Specialist do?

A Design System Specialist builds and scales reusable component libraries, design tokens, documentation and governance so that design and engineering teams ship consistent products faster. Louis Henrich introduced a design system across three brands at Swiss Marketplace Group and led a shared system adopted by 37 product teams at Rakuten Kobo.
